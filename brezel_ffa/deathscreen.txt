Qbox Medical Integration (qbx_medical)

brezel_ffa has been updated to automatically invoke:
`exports['qbx_medical']:HideDeathUI()`

This hides the Death Screen UI, Dynamic Island, and clears screen blur and movement lock when:
1. Entering an FFA lobby
2. Dying in FFA
3. Respawning / Reviving in FFA
4. Leaving FFA

brezel_ffa also exports:
`exports['brezel_ffa']:IsInFFA()`
